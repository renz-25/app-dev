# Death's Game (이재, 곧 죽습니다)

## Overview
 - **Release:** December 2023 – January 2024 (Released in 2 parts)
 - **Platform:** TVING / Amazon Prime Video
 - **Episodes:** 8 episodes
 - **Genre:** Fantasy, Dark Thriller, Action, Drama
 - **Based On:** The popular Webtoon I'll Die Soon (*Yije Got Jookseummida*) by Lee Won-sik and Ggoolchan.

## Synopsis
Choi Yi-jae is a down-on-his-luck man who has suffered through 7 years of job hunting failures, financial scammed savings, and personal loss. Overwhelmed, he decides to end his own life.

Offended by his casual view of dying, **Death** intercepts him in purgatory. As punishment for treating death as an escape, she condemns him to experience **12 different impending deaths across 12 different bodies**. If Yi-jae manages to survive in any of these body's situations, he gets to live out the remainder of that person's lifetime, otherwise, he goes straight to Hell.
